# Haidar System — Content Pillars

## Pillar 1 — Systems Thinking

Break down real problems into workflows, information, decisions, and actions.

Examples:
- Why a business needs a workflow rather than another tool.
- How to map a manual process.
- How small systems remove operational friction.

## Pillar 2 — Building in Public

Document real experiments, prototypes, architecture, implementation choices, failures, and lessons.

## Pillar 3 — AI & Automation

Show practical use of AI, agents, APIs, automation, and integrations.

## Pillar 4 — Demand & Opportunity

Explore how requests are discovered, validated, scored, and turned into action.

## Pillar 5 — Project Proof

Show actual work with context: problem, approach, system, result, lesson.

## Pillar 6 — Technology Experiments

Explore emerging technology only when there is a learning objective or real problem behind it.

## Content Quality Filter

A post should primarily do at least one:

- Teach.
- Show evidence.
- Explain a decision.
- Document an experiment.
- Reveal a useful process.
- Start a relevant conversation.

Avoid generic motivation, trend-chasing, fabricated authority, and empty technical demos.
