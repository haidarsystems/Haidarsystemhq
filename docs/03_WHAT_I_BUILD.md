# Haidar System — What I Build

Haidar System is technology-agnostic within a clear systems-building territory.

## 1. Business Systems

- Internal tools
- Dashboards
- CRM/workflow systems
- Operational systems
- Knowledge and information workflows

## 2. AI Systems

- AI-assisted workflows
- Agents and operator systems
- Research systems
- Decision-support interfaces
- AI-powered business processes

## 3. Automation & Integration

- APIs
- Webhooks
- Workflow automation
- Cross-platform integrations
- Data synchronization

## 4. Web & Digital Products

- Websites
- Landing pages
- Web applications
- Admin interfaces
- Customer-facing digital experiences

## 5. Intelligence & Opportunity Systems

- Demand discovery
- Opportunity databases
- Research pipelines
- Request/lead collection
- Scoring and prioritization
- Action workflows

## 6. Emerging Technology

Web3, blockchain, and other emerging technologies are valid exploration areas when they solve a real problem or produce meaningful learning.

## Capability Rule

The list describes capability territory, not a promise that every service is currently offered at production scale. Public claims must match actual evidence.
